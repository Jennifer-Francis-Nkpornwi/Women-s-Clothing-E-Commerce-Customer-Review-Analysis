# Business Analysis

## Overview

This folder contains the business analysis performed on the cleaned customer review dataset.

The analysis explores customer satisfaction, recommendation behavior, product performance, and review engagement through business-driven questions supported by data visualizations and insights.

Each business question is answered using appropriate charts, quantitative findings, and recommendations to support business decision-making.

---

## Analysis Scope

The analysis focuses on:

- Customer satisfaction trends
- Product recommendation patterns
- Performance across product divisions, departments, and classes
- Customer demographic analysis
- Review engagement
- Product improvement opportunities

---

## Deliverable

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Business Analysis.pdf** | Complete business analysis containing business questions, supporting visualizations, findings, insights, and recommendations. |

---

## Analytical Approach

The analysis follows a business-first approach:

1. Define the business question.
2. Select the appropriate visualization.
3. Analyze the results.
4. Extract key insights.
5. Recommend business actions where applicable.

The findings from this analysis informed the interactive dashboard, executive summary, and final business recommendations presented in this project.
