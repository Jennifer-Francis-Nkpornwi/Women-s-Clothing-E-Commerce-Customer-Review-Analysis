# Project Brief

## Project Title

**Understanding Customer Feedback in Women's Clothing E-Commerce**

---

## Background

A leading women's clothing e-commerce retailer has accumulated a large volume of customer reviews across its product catalog. These reviews include customer ratings, written feedback, recommendations, and product information. While this data contains valuable insights into customer experiences, it has not been systematically analyzed to support business decision-making.

The business requires a data-driven assessment of customer feedback to better understand customer satisfaction, identify patterns in product recommendations, and uncover opportunities for product and service improvement.

---

## Business Problem

The company wants to understand what customers are saying about its products and determine the factors that influence customer satisfaction and recommendations.

Specifically, the business seeks to:

- Identify which products receive the strongest customer feedback.
- Understand the factors driving customer recommendations.
- Recognize areas where customers express dissatisfaction.
- Use customer feedback to support product and customer experience improvements.

---

## Dataset

The analysis is based on a dataset containing **23,486 customer reviews**, with each record representing a single customer review.

### Dataset Fields

| Field | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Title** | Review title |
| **Review Text** | Customer review |
| **Rating** | Product rating (1–5) |
| **Recommended IND** | Indicates whether the customer recommends the product (1 = Yes, 0 = No) |
| **Positive Feedback Count** | Number of customers who found the review helpful |
| **Division Name** | High-level product division |
| **Department Name** | Product department |
| **Class Name** | Product class |

---

## Project Objectives

The objective of this analysis is to explore customer review data and generate actionable insights that help the business better understand customer experiences.

The analysis will:

- Assess overall customer satisfaction using review ratings.
- Examine customer recommendation patterns.
- Identify trends across product divisions, departments, and classes.
- Develop meaningful business questions that support decision-making.
- Present findings using clear visualizations and supporting analysis.

---

## Scope of Work

The project includes:

- Cleaning and preparing the dataset for analysis.
- Identifying and resolving data quality issues where appropriate.
- Performing exploratory data analysis to answer business-driven questions.
- Developing an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate key findings.
- Producing an executive summary with recommendations supported by the analysis.

---

## Expected Deliverables

The completed project will include:

- A cleaned and analysis-ready dataset.
- Business questions supported by data, visualizations, and insights.
- An interactive Power BI dashboard presenting key findings.
- An executive summary summarizing the analysis and business recommendations.
