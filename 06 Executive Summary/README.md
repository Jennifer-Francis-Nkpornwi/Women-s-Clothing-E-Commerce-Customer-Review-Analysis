# Executive Summary

## Overview

This folder contains the executive summary prepared at the conclusion of the customer review analysis.

The summary presents the key findings from the analysis in a concise, business-focused format, highlighting the most significant insights and strategic recommendations for management.

Designed for decision-makers, it provides a high-level overview of the project without requiring a review of the complete analysis or dashboard.

---

## Purpose

The executive summary aims to:

- Summarize the key findings from the analysis.
- Highlight the most important business insights.
- Present evidence-based recommendations.
- Support informed business decision-making.

---

## Deliverable

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Executive Summary.md** | A concise summary of the analysis, key findings, and business recommendations prepared for management. |

---

## Summary Scope

The executive summary includes:

- Business context
- Key analytical findings
- Major business insights
- Strategic recommendations
- Overall conclusion

---

## Notes

The executive summary is based on the findings from the business analysis and the interactive dashboard developed for this project. It is intended for stakeholders who require a concise overview of the analysis and its business implications.
