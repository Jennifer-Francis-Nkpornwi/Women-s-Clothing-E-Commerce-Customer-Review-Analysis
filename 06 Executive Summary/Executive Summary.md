# Executive Summary

This analysis examined **23,486 customer reviews** from the company's women's clothing e-commerce platform to understand customer satisfaction, identify the factors influencing product recommendations, and uncover opportunities to improve product quality and the overall customer experience. The analysis incorporated customer ratings, recommendation behavior, product categories, review sentiment, and customer feedback to generate actionable business insights.

Overall, customer satisfaction is strong, with an **average product rating of 4.2 out of 5** and a **recommendation rate of approximately 82%**, indicating that most customers are satisfied with their purchases and are willing to recommend the company's products. A strong positive relationship was observed between customer ratings and recommendation behavior, with customers awarding higher ratings significantly more likely to recommend products to others. This demonstrates that improving customer satisfaction directly contributes to stronger customer advocacy and brand loyalty.

Analysis by product department showed that the **Bottoms**, **Intimate**, and **Jackets** departments achieved the highest average customer ratings, while the **Trend** department recorded the lowest satisfaction scores, highlighting an opportunity for targeted product improvement. Product class analysis further revealed that **Dresses (6,319 reviews)**, **Knits (4,843 reviews)**, and **Blouses (3,097 reviews)** generated the highest customer engagement, making them the company's most influential product categories.

Although these product classes also recorded the highest numbers of negative reviews, this reflects their larger review volumes. A more meaningful performance indicator, the **Negative Review Rate**, showed that **Blouses (11.2%)** recorded the highest proportion of negative reviews, followed by **Dresses (10.9%)** and **Knits (10.4%)**. This suggests that monitoring complaint rates rather than complaint counts provides a more accurate measure of product performance.

Qualitative analysis of customer reviews identified recurring themes relating to **fit, sizing consistency, fabric quality, comfort, and overall product appearance**. These factors consistently appeared in lower-rated reviews and represent the primary drivers of customer dissatisfaction.

## Recommendations

Based on these findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. **Prioritize product quality improvements** for **Dresses, Knits, and Blouses**, with particular attention to fit accuracy, sizing consistency, fabric quality, and product descriptions. Improving these high-volume product classes is likely to have the greatest impact on customer satisfaction and reduce negative reviews.

2. **Establish a continuous customer feedback monitoring process** that tracks customer ratings, recommendation behavior, sentiment, and the **Negative Review Rate** by product class. Monitoring these key performance indicators will enable the company to identify emerging product issues early, support data-driven product improvements, increase customer recommendations, and strengthen long-term customer loyalty.
