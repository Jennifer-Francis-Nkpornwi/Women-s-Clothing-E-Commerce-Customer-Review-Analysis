# Women's Clothing E-Commerce Customer Review Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes **23,486 customer reviews** from a women's clothing e-commerce retailer to uncover the factors influencing customer satisfaction, product recommendations, and overall shopping experience.

Using **Power BI**, **Power Query**, **Excel**, and **DAX**, the project transforms raw customer feedback into actionable business insights that can support product improvement, merchandising decisions, and customer experience initiatives.

---

## Business Problem

The company receives thousands of customer reviews containing valuable feedback on its products. However, without structured analysis, it is difficult to determine:

- Which products customers love most.
- What drives product recommendations.
- Which product categories require improvement.
- How customer feedback can guide better business decisions.

This project addresses these challenges by analyzing customer ratings, recommendations, product categories, and review engagement.

---

## Project Objectives

The analysis aims to:

- Assess overall customer satisfaction.
- Understand factors influencing customer recommendations.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming product categories.
- Explore customer behavior across product divisions and departments.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve customer experience and product performance.

---

## Dataset Overview

**Dataset Size:** 23,486 Customer Reviews

### Key Variables

| Column | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Rating | Product rating (1–5) |
| Recommended IND | Whether the customer recommends the product |
| Positive Feedback Count | Number of helpful votes received |
| Division Name | Product division |
| Department Name | Product department |
| Class Name | Product class |
| Age | Customer age |
| Title | Review title |
| Review Text | Customer review |

## Business Questions

This analysis answers key business questions, including:

- What is the overall customer satisfaction level?
- What percentage of customers recommend the products?
- Which product divisions receive the highest ratings?
- Which departments generate the most negative feedback?
- Which product classes are most recommended?
- Which customer age groups are the most satisfied?
- Do longer reviews receive more positive feedback?
- Which products should management prioritize for improvement?
- Which departments generate the highest volume of customer reviews?
- What factors appear to influence customer recommendations?

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, recommendations focus on:

- Improving low-performing product categories.
- Strengthening quality assurance for poorly rated products.
- Leveraging high-performing products in marketing campaigns.
- Using customer feedback to guide product development.
- Encouraging more detailed customer reviews to improve future decision-making.
